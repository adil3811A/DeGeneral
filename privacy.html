DeGeneral Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

DeGeneral ("DeGeneral", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") is a private journaling application developed by Adil.

This Privacy Policy explains how DeGeneral handles information when you use the app.

Privacy at a glance DeGeneral is designed to keep your journal and AI conversations on your device.
  • No account is required.
  • Journal entries are stored locally on your device.
  • Chat conversations are stored locally on your device.
  • The AI model runs locally on your device.
  • Your writing is not sent to an AI API or DeGeneral server.
  • DeGeneral does not provide cloud synchronization.
  • DeGeneral does not sell your personal information.
  • DeGeneral does not display advertising.
  • The network is used during setup to download the AI model.

1. Information stored by DeGeneral

Journal entries

When you create a journal entry, DeGeneral may store information you provide, including:

  • Entry title
  • Entry text
  • Mood
  • Date
  • Tags

This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your Android device.

Chat conversations

When you use the AI Chat feature, your messages and the AI's responses are stored locally on your device so your conversation can be displayed again.

Your journal entries and chat conversations are not uploaded to DeGeneral servers.

Application preferences

DeGeneral stores certain preferences locally on your device, such as your selected appearance mode (System, Light, or Dark).

2. On-device AI processing

DeGeneral uses Gemma 3 1B, provided by Google DeepMind, for features such as:

  • Grammar and spelling refinement
  • Title suggestions
  • AI conversation
  • Deeper reflection

The model runs locally on your device using your device's CPU.

Text processed by these AI features is not sent to a remote AI service by DeGeneral.

AI responses may be inaccurate or inappropriate. AI responses should not be treated as professional medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other expert advice.

3. Model download

During initial setup, DeGeneral downloads the AI model from a remote hosting service.

The model download is approximately 806 MB.

The network connection is used to obtain the model. Your journal content and chat conversations are not uploaded as part of this download.

The downloaded model is verified using a SHA-256 checksum before DeGeneral uses it. If the downloaded file does not match the expected checksum, it is deleted and is not loaded by the app.

4. Information we do not collect

DeGeneral does not require or collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera data
  • Journal content on a remote server
  • Chat conversations on a remote server

DeGeneral does not use journal content for advertising or user profiling.

5. Data sharing

DeGeneral does not sell, rent, or share your journal entries or chat conversations with third parties.

The AI processing performed by DeGeneral occurs on your device rather than through a remote AI API.

The application does contact the network during the initial model download. This connection is used to download the required AI model.

6. Data security

DeGeneral stores journal entries and conversations in the application's private storage on your Android device.

DeGeneral does not add its own encryption layer to journal data.

The application relies on Android's security mechanisms for app-private storage and the device's available encryption protections.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also protect your device with appropriate screen-lock and device-security settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your journal entries and chat conversations remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application's local data.

You can delete individual journal entries using the app.

You can clear the chat conversation using the Clear Chat function.

Because DeGeneral does not maintain a remote account or cloud copy of your journal, there is no DeGeneral server-side account containing your journal data to delete.

Uninstalling DeGeneral or clearing its application data may remove locally stored application data according to Android's behavior and your device settings.

8. Children's privacy

DeGeneral is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a remote service because DeGeneral does not operate a remote account or user-data collection service.

9. Third-party software

DeGeneral uses open-source and third-party software components to provide its functionality, including:

  • Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform
  • Room
  • Llamatik
  • llama.cpp
  • Gemma 3 1B

These components are used to provide local application functionality. DeGeneral does not use them to create a remote account or upload your journal content to DeGeneral servers.

The Gemma model is provided under Google's Gemma Terms of Use.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when DeGeneral's functionality or data practices change.

When the policy is updated, the effective date at the beginning of the policy will be changed.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DeGeneral's privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the DeGeneral GitHub repository.
